# Style DNA

## In one line

Warm cream paper, minimal, hand-drawn, generous white space, restrained, absurd, product-sketch feel, notebook aesthetic, structure clear but not instructional.

Like a quick explanatory sketch drawn on a ruled notebook page by someone who works deeply in AI, product design, and developer tools.

## Must-haves

- 16:9 horizontal article illustration.
- Warm cream paper background: use #faf7f1 (Paper & Pencil --paper). No pure white, no beige gradients, no paper textures that look scanned, no shadows, no noise, no vintage paper feel beyond the natural warmth of the cream.
- Near-black hand-drawn line art: use #1b1a17 (Paper & Pencil --ink). Fine lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector, not heavy outlines.
- Generous empty space: main subject occupies roughly 40%–60% of the canvas, at least 35% left as open cream space, ideally with one large quiet region.
- Sparse handwritten annotations: at most 5–8, each kept to 2–8 words.
- One image explains only one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Structure should emerge naturally — do not write the structure-type name on the image.

## Paper & Pencil Theme Color Palette

Match these colors from the site's Paper & Pencil CSS theme:

| Role | Hex | CSS token | Usage |
|------|-----|-----------|-------|
| Paper background | `#faf7f1` | `--paper` | Image background |
| Main ink / line art | `#1b1a17` | `--ink` | Xiaohei, outlines, structure lines |
| Primary flow / arrows | `#8a5a1f` | `--accent` | Main path, arrows, primary direction |
| Accent soft fill | `rgba(138,90,31,0.08)` | `--accent-soft` | Light fill areas |
| Warning / problem | `#c04848` | (margin-line family) | Errors, pitfalls, blockers |
| Secondary notes | `#466480` | (paper-line family) | Supplementary labels, system state |
| Structural rules | `#b9b19b` | `--rule-strong` | Supporting lines, dividers |

**Dark mode variant** (when generating for dark theme, `[data-theme="dark"]`): invert to `--paper: #16140f` background and `--ink: #ebe4d2` line art, with `--accent: #d6a865` for the warm amber flow color.

## Color Use Rules

- **Near-black (#1b1a17)**: Xiaohei, main outlines, frames, structure, primary text, main objects.
- **Sepia/amber (#8a5a1f)**: Primary flow, path, arrows, the main A→B movement — this replaces the original orange.
- **Muted red (#c04848)**: Key annotations, problems, emotional beats, critical warnings, results.
- **Muted blue (#466480)**: Supplementary notes, internal state, system state, second-layer explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue is not required in every image. Keep color use restrained — less is better.

## Never

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographics.
- No formal flowcharts.
- No course slides.
- No cute cartoon posters.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagrams.
- No polished flat-design illustration.
- No tech-UI feel.
- No real app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- Don't explain every node.
- No title in the top-left corner ("Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap").

## Aesthetic Direction

Absurd, creative, interesting, clean and airy, imaginative.

Not cute, not childish, not overcrowded, not rigid.
